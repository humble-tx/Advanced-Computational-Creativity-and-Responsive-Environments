# Practice-led Research

## 1. 什么是“基于实践的研究”？ (What is it?)

### 观点 1：艺术即研究 (Art IS Research)
- Brad Haseman 提出“展演性研究”(Performative research)。艺术作品本身就是以象征形式表达的研究成果。
- Brad Haseman calls it "Performative research". The artwork itself embodies research findings expressed symbolically.

### 观点 2：实践生发认知 (Practice LEADS TO Insights)
- Barbara Bolt 强调“实践性知识”(Praxical knowledge)。通过“亲自动手处理材料”产生独特认知，并转化为理论。
- Barbara Bolt emphasizes "Praxical knowledge". Handling materials in practice generates specific insights that can be theorised.

### 观点 3：全景视角 (The Comprehensive View)
- 综合定义：艺术作品本身 + 创作过程的记录与理论化 = 完整的研究。
- Combined view: The artwork itself + documentation/theorisation of the process = complete research.

## 2. My Perspective

### ✅ Agree

#### 实践出真知 (Irreplaceable Praxical Knowledge)
- 从实践中推导理论，与把理论硬套在实践上，是完全不同的思维。纯靠读书无法替代动手试错。
- Theorising out of practice is entirely different from applying theory to practice. Hands-on trial and error cannot be replaced by reading alone.

#### 迭代循环网 (Iterative Cyclic Web)
- 研究与实践并非孤立，它们在系统中来回交织、循环发展。
- Research and practice are interwoven in a dynamic cyclic web.

---

### (Reservations)

#### 纯艺术品 = 完整学术研究？(Pure artwork = Complete rigorous research?)
- 很难！标准学术知识需要“可推广性”(Generalisable) 和“可转移性”(Transferable)。单一作品往往过于模糊多义。
- Unlikely! Standard research must be generalisable and transferable. A single artwork is often too ambiguous.

#### 文字记录 / 理论化不可或缺
- 同意 Bolt 与编辑的观点：“只有实践”会削弱研究价值。必须把隐性直觉转化为显性记录，才能真正贡献知识体系。
- Agree with Bolt and editors: "Practice-only" can weaken research. Personal intuition must be documented explicitly to contribute to knowledge.
